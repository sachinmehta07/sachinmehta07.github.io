Meet EzyMember.

An offline-first membership tracker for gyms, tutors, and salons. Add a member in two clicks, send a WhatsApp renewal reminder in one, back up privately to your own Google Drive, and hide financials when staff need the list but not the numbers.

Explore EzyMember →
Tired of scrolling through messy records to see who's expiring?

Filter by 1, 2, 3 weeks, or a month — see exactly who's coming up for renewal, instantly.

Want a clear view of who needs a follow-up today?

Members sort themselves into Active, Expiring Soon, and Expired, so you always know exactly where you stand.

Still typing the same renewal message by hand, every time?

Renewal reminders, expiry alerts, even welcome messages — every one pre-filled and ready. Preview, tweak if needed, and send in a single tap.

Need to share your member list without exposing your revenue?

Export a member's full history — every plan, date, and payment — to PDF or Excel. Toggle financials off for staff-safe reports whenever they need to be shared.
